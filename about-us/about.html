Our Mission

To accelerate the global workforce adoption of coworking through discovery, bookings, and frictionless access.

We believe when people access inspiring and collaborative workspaces, good things happen. In 2015, Coworker was founded in order to help accelerate the adoption of coworking, by enabling the discovery of coworking spaces around the world. We envision a future where on-demand access to flexible workspace enables 1 billion people to participate in coworking annually. We believe this event will drive innovation and society forward, faster. Every day we work towards developing our technology and network to support this bright future that we will all share.
